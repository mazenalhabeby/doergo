How these ads are designed

Hook in the first 2 seconds

Meta says about half an ad's value lands in the first 3 seconds. We open on the pain ("Where is Mike?"), not the logo. Movement starts in frame one.

Big word-by-word captions

Heavy capitals, one highlighted word per line in HBCField blue, placed in the area TikTok and Reels keep free of their own buttons.

Real screens, moving camera

Slow push-ins and pans across the real app, a phone tilted in 3D, notification cards that spring in, pins that drop on the map, numbers that count up.

A clear offer at the end

"Try it free for 14 days" and "From €9.99 per person". A low price in an expensive category is itself a hook.

Before I record the paid voiceover

Four things to decide

1 · Music

I can generate a track with ElevenLabs Music if your key allows it, or use a royalty-free track you choose. Ads without music perform badly on TikTok and Reels.

2 · Languages

English first. German versions for Austria and Germany are easy to add with the same voice.

3 · Voice

Your usual narrator is calm, which suits Ad B. Ad A may want a more energetic voice. I can make a 10-second sample of each.

4 · "No credit card"

Adding "No card needed" to the offer usually lifts signups. Only if the trial really starts without a card.
