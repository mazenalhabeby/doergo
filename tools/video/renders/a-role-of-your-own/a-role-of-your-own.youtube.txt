TITLE
A role of your own — HBCField

DESCRIPTION
Every company has a job that no product's built-in roles describe — the person who books the work, the one who chases paperwork, the foreman who is not a manager. Write the role, tick what it may do, give it to somebody. It takes about a minute, and it is the difference between the product fitting your company and your company fitting the product.
