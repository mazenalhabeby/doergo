A company, or a person — HBCField

DESCRIPTION
You invoice a company, but you ring a person. HBCField keeps both, and links them: the people you speak to at a client are records of their own, with a job title and a place in the company — and they are never counted as clients, because six contacts at one firm are not six customers.
