TITLE
Worked and counted — HBCField

DESCRIPTION
HBCField keeps two clocks on every shift. One is what happened — the times somebody actually clocked in and out, kept as evidence and never edited to suit anybody. The other is what counts: the shift's own hours, less the rests that do not count. They are rarely the same number, and both are right.
