# HBCField — Intro Video

## Long description / voiceover (~90–120s)

- **Goal:** Introduce HBCField and make companies feel *"this solves my daily chaos."*
- **Audience:** Owners and operations managers of field-service businesses (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, maintenance, installation, facilities).
- **Tone:** Confident, warm, plain-spoken. Problem → solution → outcomes.

---

### Full narration

> **[The problem]**
> Running a field team is a juggling act. Jobs come in by phone, text, and email. Your office is on the radio trying to reach technicians. Paperwork piles up. Timesheets are guesswork. And at the end of the day, you're still not sure who did what, where they were, or whether the job's actually done. Your office and your field are working hard — just not *together*.

> **[The solution]**
> That's exactly why we built **HBCField** — one platform that finally connects your office and your field team, from the first job request to the signed-off report.

> **[Create & dispatch]**
> Create a job in seconds, add the details and the location, and assign the right technician for the work. They're notified instantly — no phone tag. From your desk, you see every job move through its stages, live: assigned, on the way, arrived, in progress, done.

> **[The field experience]**
> Your technicians work entirely from their phone. They accept the job, navigate straight to the site, capture before-and-after photos, add notes, and close it out with a professional service report — signed by the customer, right on the screen. No clipboards, no lost paperwork, no data entry back at the office.

> **[Live visibility & GPS]**
> And you're never in the dark. A live map shows where your team is and the exact route they drove to each job — so you can answer "when will someone arrive?" with confidence, not a shrug.

> **[Time & attendance]**
> HBCField also handles time and attendance. Your team clocks in and out with location verification, breaks are tracked, and overtime is flagged — so payroll takes minutes, not a lost afternoon, and every hour is accounted for.

> **[Scheduling]**
> Plan the week with a clear view of who's working, who's available, and who's on time off — and approve leave requests in a tap.

> **[One place]**
> Task management, GPS tracking, time and attendance, scheduling, and reporting — together in one platform, instead of scattered across five different apps and a stack of spreadsheets.

> **[The outcomes — the "why it matters"]**
> The result? Less time on admin, and more time on the work that pays. Faster dispatch, so you fit more jobs into every day. Reports and signatures on the spot, so you invoice sooner and get paid faster. Real accountability, because everything's timestamped and on the record. And customers who are impressed — because you're on time, professional, and always in the loop.

> **[Close]**
> Whether you run a team of five or fifty, HBCField gives you back control of your day. **Your office and your field, finally in sync. Less admin. More done.** HBCField — field service, simplified.
