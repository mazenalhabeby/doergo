(01) — What it is

Running a field team shouldn't take three apps and a spreadsheet.

Most field businesses run dispatch in one tool, time-tracking in another, and GPS in a third — then reconcile them by hand. HBCField is one system that does all three, split cleanly across a web portal for the office and a mobile app for the field.

01 / OFFICE

The web portal — dispatch & oversight

Admins and dispatchers create jobs, assign the right technician, watch the live map, approve hours and reports, and plan across every site — all in real time.

02 / FIELD

The mobile app — execution

Technicians see their day the moment they open it: accept and complete jobs, clock in on site, capture photos and signatures, and follow the route — online or on the move.
