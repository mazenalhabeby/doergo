    What changes from today's app
    • One design. Every job screen moves to the design the home screen already uses. Today none of them do.
    • The list answers “what next”. Next up is the same card as the home widget, with the real button, not an “Open” button. Below it, jobs are grouped Doing now, Blocked, Overdue, Today, Later.
    • The job keeps one button at the bottom. The next step, a Navigate or Call disc, and ⋯ for everything else. Decline and Report an issue move into ⋯, so nobody declines by accident.
    • Checklist items can be ticked. Today they are read-only.
    • Finishing is three short steps (what you did, photos, sign-off) instead of one long form.
    • Every popup is the same bottom sheet. Today the job screen uses four raw pop-ups that skip the one-at-a-time rule, which is how buttons went dead on the shift card.
    • Photos and comments become one activity feed, with status changes in it.
    Needs the backend later (not in this build)

    A client phone number for Call, ticking checklist items on the server, and a view of a finished service report. The mockup shows where they go; without the data they stay hidden.
