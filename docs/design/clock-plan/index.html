Rule 1

The past stays exactly as it is

No old entry is rewritten — not its times, not its labels, not even a wrong one. The new rules apply from the day of the update, to new entries only.

The worker’s day

Smarter and lighter — as approved

Arrive

One card, one tap — or none with the automatic clock. A refusal offers “Ask to clock in from here”.

Lunch off site

Leaving inside the planned rest window is the rest. No “you left the work area”.

End of shift

One question: Clock out, or Ask for more time. A “no” never clocks anyone out.

Forgot to clock out

“You left at 16:42 — clock out then?” from the moment the phone saw them leave.

Fewer pushes

At most four clock pushes a day; a reminder repeats only if the first wasn’t opened.

The office

One item per person per day; the worker hears the decision.
