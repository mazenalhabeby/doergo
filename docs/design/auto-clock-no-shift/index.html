    Choices in this design

    • Chosen per person in a workspace, next to "By his shift". Someone who also has rota days keeps clocking by the shift on those days, and by arriving and leaving on the others.
    • Times are recorded from the real moment: arriving, and leaving. The 10 and 20 minutes are only how long the phone waits to be sure.
    • Outside his usual hours the phone asks "Starting work at AGRU?" instead of clocking. No answer within 15 minutes = nothing recorded.
    • An automatic clock-in can be undone for 10 minutes, as today. The office sees the steps marked as automatic.
    • A rest is not placed automatically, because there is no planned shift to centre it in. After 6 hours the phone asks if he took one; the workspace's missed-rest rule applies as today.
    • The longest session is not a number of its own: it is the workspace's hours without a shift (8, 10, 12 — whatever AGRU sets), the same number its long-session reminder already uses. Past it he is asked; with the workspace on "Allow up to a number of hours", time past it counts only when approved as overtime.
    • A shift closed for review ends at the last moment the phone saw him on site, never at the limit.
