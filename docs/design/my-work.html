The strip is navigation
A bar is a shift, dots are jobs, the ring is the day being shown. Tapping a day changes the list in place — that interaction is the whole reason ⑤ folded in rather than sitting above.
The first row grows
It carries the verb its status allows and a way to reach the person. Everything below it stays a plain row. No second card, so nothing to keep in sync.
Mine always leads
Every face that shows other people’s work shows the reader’s first, grouped and marked. Solid dot = yours. Hollow ring = somebody else’s, with their name.
The card is not a target
Only its parts: a day, a row, the header pill. Selecting a day is a read — §4 forbids writing on render and this does not.
One read, not three
Jobs come through useHomeTasks() — never tasksApi.list(). Week dots are task dates already in hand. Only the rota is an extra call, and only for people who have one.
It is never hidden
Once the gate is tasks OR clock, anybody with either always has it — so every dead end has to be a written sentence rather than a disappearance.

06The marks
Drawn for this row, in the construction glyphs.tsx already describes: one hue at four opacities, and the detail punched out of the solid mark. Not outlines — an outline has no mass at 19px inside a 46px disc, which is the size these are read at, outdoors, at arm’s length.
Accept
A job card with the check cut through it. One object — the card is the acceptance.
On my way
A van going somewhere. The arches are bitten out of the body so the wheels read as wheels, and the two marks behind it are the difference between a van and on my way.
I have arrived
A solid pin standing on its own shadow. The shadow is what makes it arrived rather than a place.
Start work
The same disc as Break on the shift card, a different mark inside. Family, not repetition.
Finish
A seal, three rings deep. Weight is what makes it final — a check on its own is just another tick.
Call
A handset ringing. The two arcs are the difference between a phone that exists and a call being made.
Navigate
The arrow solid, with its fold line punched out rather than drawn.
More
Three dots. The one mark in the set that should not be reinvented.
⚠️ contrast is the surface, not a colour choice
It is punched OUT of a solid shape, so it must be the exact ground the mark sits on — a lighter jade inside the filled button, the tile colour on a disc. Wrong value does not error; it draws a grey ghost across the shape.
The verb is data, the mark is not
Labels come from FlowStatus.label and are organization-authored. The marks map to the transition, not the wording — so a workflow that calls it “Roll out” still gets the van.
Never alone
Every disc keeps a text label beside or beneath it. Icons never carry meaning by themselves, and the jade button says Accept in words as well as in the mark.
