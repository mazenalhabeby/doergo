The job — the whole flow

Every step of the real Field Service workflow, the issue report and the sign-off. Every action comes from a transition, every widget from a capability — never from a step name, which is the whole of §04 in the workflow reference.

AND THE REST OF IT
✕Reassign and Edit sit above the primary.Two outlined buttons, full width, on every step — and they are a manager's actions, not the member's. They belong in More.
✕“Location Tracking Active” is a solid green bar.It looks like the most important button on the screen and it is not a button at all. It is a fact — so it is a chip.
✕Five status colours from outside the system.#2563EB #7c3aed #CA8A04 #16A34A as pale pills. They come from FlowStatus.color and they are not Amazen — the tone should come from what the status means.
✕Report Issue is a centre modal with a red button.Red is destructive. Telling your manager something is wrong is the responsible act, and the app should not make it look like deleting something.
✕The completion form asks for everything, every time.Summary, work details, 5 before photos, 5 after photos, two signatures and a customer name — on an internal HVAC job at Main Office Floor 2 with no customer. §03: “Every field is shown every time, whatever the job.”
✕Three required fields with no way to know it.SUMMARY *, both signatures and the name are starred, and you find out by pressing Complete Job.
✕The progress rail has no labels.Six dots and one word under the current one. You cannot see what is next, which is the only question worth asking on this screen.

⑧The rules
THE RULES
one primaryThe next step, and nothing else is filled.Label from the transition, so a custom workflow reads “Next: On site” only if nobody set a button label. Reassign, Edit, Open in Maps and the history all live under More.
never a nameEvery widget comes from a capability, every action from a transition.§04 lists seven behaviours keyed to English step names. This flow reads capabilities and transitions only, so a workflow called “On the road” behaves exactly like one called EN_ROUTE.
the timerFrom the first step where timer is on. Never from creation.2h 41m, not 137 days. And it is set like every other figure in the app — integer at 52, unit at 24.
status toneFrom meaning, not from FlowStatus.color.Jade while it is going to plan, warn when something needs a person, crit only when it is blocked. Three tones, not one per step.
facts are chips“Travel is being tracked”, “2h 41m on the job”, “3 photos”.Never a full-width filled bar. A member should never need to know the step has gps to understand they are being located.
the issueNot red, not a modal, and always available.A sheet with six tappable reasons and an optional photo. It says who it reaches and that the job is not cancelled. Today it is tied to a BLOCKED step, which none of your workflows has.
finishingAsk only for what the step's capabilities name.A step with signature asks for one signature. Two signatures and a customer name only when the job has a customer. One required field, and it is one line.
the receiptA finished job ends in a record, like a shift does.Started, finishing, checklist, photos, counted — mono, right-aligned, a rule above the total. Same language as the clock-out sheet, because it is the same kind of moment.
