③ Today’s route — the line, not the map

A map answers where. A driver at 13:12 is asking how much is left and when they get home. So the route is drawn as a line whose gaps are as tall as the drives are long — and the past folds up as the day goes on.

①The idea
A map answers where. Standing in a car park at 13:12, nobody is asking where. They are asking how much is left and when they get home.
THE LINE IS TIME
The gap between two stops is as tall as the drive is long. 31 minutes is twice 14. You read the shape of the day before you read a word — a long haul after lunch, three quick ones before it.
THE PAST COMPRESSES
Done stops collapse to a row of ticks. The card is the same height at 08:00 and at 16:00 — the future always gets the room, because the future is the part you can still change.
And the hero is 16:40 — when you finish, recomputed at every stop. No map shows that number, and it is the one a driver actually wants.

⑧The rules
THE RULES
height = timeGap height is the drive, clamped 20–62pt.Below 20 two stops touch; above 62 one motorway run eats the card. Inside that range it is honest, and the shape of the day is free.
you are in the gapWhile driving, the dot sits between two stations, not on one.Because that is where you are. The gap label becomes the countdown — 12m to go — and it is the only thing on the card that moves.
late bends forwardWhen you are behind, every stop after you turns warn and shows its old time.“16:08 · was 15:20”. A driver who is 48m late needs to know which promises just broke, and to whom — not that they are late, which they already know.
the past foldsThree done stops become three ticks and one line.Tapping unfolds them. A finished stop is a record, not a task, and records belong in the history, not in today.
one actionNavigate to the next one. Nothing else is a button.The route has one obvious verb. Reorder, skip and call live on the stop itself, one tap deeper.
no map, everNot even a small one.A thumbnail map is unreadable at 60pt, costs a tile fetch on mobile data, and answers a question nobody has. Navigate hands off to the phone's own maps, which is what a driver will use anyway.
finishing timeRecomputed at every arrival and departure, never on a timer.It moves when something real happened. A number that drifts while the phone sits in a cup holder is a number nobody trusts twice.
