The smart part · 2

It recognises what is being asked, and checks it before it reaches you

A member who writes free text in "Other" gets the right kind suggested — from the organization's own kinds and its words, on the phone, no AI service. Before the request arrives, the system already checked what a leader would check.

Try: "can I stay 2 more hours tonight", "fuel 84 euro for the van", "working from home tomorrow", "new boots size 43".

Within each group the oldest comes first. A request starting soon moves up on its own as the day comes closer.

To confirm

Choices in this design

  • All 13 kinds come in: shift hours, away from site, extra time, overtime, leave and other requests, no-shows, shift issues, expenses, asset proposals, documents to verify, new people, client requests, guest requests.
  • Approve all only for shift hours (as today). Everything else is decided one by one, because each needs its own look.
  • Reject always asks a reason, and the member reads it.
  • "Delegate": a leader going on leave hands their inbox to someone for those days.
  • The two gaps close as part of this: extra time on the web, and expenses, proposals, documents and away-from-site on the phone.
  • Routing is read from what exists (workspace, leaders and watchers, permissions, who is on leave); the office only adds rules: thresholds (hours, money, cover), a second step, and how long before it moves on.
  • Every item says why you see it and who else can decide; a request reaches nobody who has no business with it — colleagues never see each other's requests.
  • Detection suggests a kind from the words and fills what it can; the member always confirms. No AI service: it runs on the phone, from the organization's own kinds.
  • Later, the custom request types (parked) arrive in the same inbox with no extra work.
