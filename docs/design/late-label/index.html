In the code · not in your data yet

Three ways an on-time worker will be shown “Late”

Back from lunch

Shift 08:00–16:00. On time at 07:58, clocks out at 12:00, back in at 13:00. The 13:00 entry is compared with 08:00.

→ “Late 300 min”, sent to review

Two shifts in a day

08:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00. Back at 12:05 for the afternoon: matched to the morning shift.

→ “Late 245 min”, wrong end and reminders

Night, then day

22:00–06:00 and 07:00–15:00. Arrives 06:05 for the day shift: matched to last night’s.

→ “Late ~8 h”

Two other screens count differently

Reports count “late” from 1 minute with no grace, and add every entry. The No-shows record always uses 10 minutes.

→ numbers that disagree with the label
The fixes

Five changes, each proven by a test

1

An office edit keeps “Late”

When the clock-in is still past the grace minutes after the edit.

2

Two clock-ins arriving together make one entry

An automatic and a manual clock-in for the same shift join; no 0-minute “Left early”.

3

Only the first clock-in of a shift can be “Late”

Coming back after lunch, or after a short trip, never is.

4

A clock-in matches the shift it falls in

Or the next one to start — never a shift that is already over.

5

Reports and No-shows count late like the label

Same grace minutes, only the first entry of each shift.
