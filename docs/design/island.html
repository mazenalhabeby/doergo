The island — everything

The in-app notification, complete. The camera covers nothing — the slots are measured, the text is measured, and the first rung that fits wins. Nine cameras, five tones, both modes, and the motion that carries it.

①What it is
A black capsule that wraps the camera. It is not a push notification — no OS permission, no entitlement, no APNs. It is a view in the root layout, so it behaves identically on both platforms.
WHY THIS ONE
While you are on the clock it never leaves — a ring and a running figure. Every screen in the app inherits a live shift clock for free.
THE ONE LAW
The hole plus 6pt each side is not a layout area. Every other rule exists to serve this one.

⑨The rules
THE RULES
the lens winsThe camera covers nothing, ever.If a phone reports a 126pt island, that is 138pt of width simply gone. The design works around it, never under it.
never cutNo ellipsis, no clipping, no shrink-to-fit.A message worth interrupting someone for is worth reading. If it does not fit, the island gets bigger.
measure textonTextLayout, never a character count.German and French run 30–40% longer than English. A string that is rung 2 in English is rung 3 in German. All six locales get measured.
lens pinnedLeft and right grow at the same rate.Anchor to the hole’s centre, not the box’s. If the camera appears to slide, the illusion dies in one frame.
decide oncePick the rung, then animate. Never re-measure mid-flight.Text re-wrapping during a 420ms morph is what makes cheap versions look cheap.
always blackLight mode changes nothing — not the island, not its ink.It is hardware, not a surface. A white pill in light mode is just a toast again.
one islandA second message replaces the first. The live shift always wins the collapsed state.It is one piece of hardware. Two would destroy the illusion instantly.
the cap356pt wide, 8pt from either edge, 3 lines of body.Past that it is not a notification, it is a screen. The fourth line becomes “Open”.
three shapeswrap a floating cutout · dock under one on the bezel · rect when there is none.You cannot wrap something bolted to the top edge, and trying looks like a bug.
motion offCross-fade between sizes over 180ms. No spring, no travel, no overshoot.And announceForAccessibility on every arrival — the current toast announces nothing at all.
