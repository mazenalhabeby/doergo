③Six lines
Same box, same 54pt. Only the words change.
A · THE CLOCKthe count, and the sharpest fact under it
33 things need youThe oldest has waited 11 days›
The 11 days is what makes it uncomfortable to leave. Works whether or not anything blocks work, and it is true every day it is true.
B · THE STAKEthe consequence, not the task
22 things are stopping your workNothing new reaches you until they are done›
The strongest, but only honest when blocksWork is true. Use it when it is, fall back to A when it is not.
C · THE PERSONa name is harder to ignore than a number
3Anna is waiting on 3 thingsSent back one of them 4 days ago›
Same instinct as ⑤ Needs you — “a name is harder to leave alone”. ⚠️ Needs a reviewer name on the wire, and falls apart when the three came from three people.
D · THE EFFORTshrink the job, not the count
33 things need youAbout two minutes, all of them›
Good for uploads, a lie for anything that needs a scan or a signature, and there is no field to estimate from. Charming and unbuildable.
E · THE FINISHprogress framing, and it names them
33 to go before you are clearedID card · licence · contract›
Reads well and the three names make it concrete. ⚠️ “Cleared” has no meaning in this app — nothing else uses the word, and three labels overflow on a narrow phone.
F · THE SHARPEST + THE RESTlead with the worst one, count the rest
3Your ID card has waited 11 daysAnd 2 more things need you›
The most specific of the six. ⚠️ But it buries the count in the second line, and a member who fixes the ID card still has a strip — which is the “app looks broken” problem again.
④The three tones

warn is the normal one. crit only when work is actually stopped, and then the line changes to say so. When the only items are expiring documents there is no tint at all — a mention, not a demand.
⑤The rules
THE RULES OF THE LINE
one boxNo stack, no badge, no peeking cards.The stack was a visual answer to a copy problem. One box, 54pt, and the words do the work.
the count is the markThe figure sits where the glyph sits, at 19px, in the tone.It counts without a badge, it is unmistakably a number of things, and a member sees 3 before they read a word.
never the taskWith one item, name it. With several, never name one.“Upload your ID card + 2” makes a member fix the ID card and expect the strip to go. It does not, and the app looks broken.
the second line earns the tapIt carries the single sharpest fact about the set.The oldest age, or the consequence. Not “tap to see more” — a line that only describes the tap is a line doing no work.
B when true, A when notblocksWork → the stake. Otherwise → the clock.Two lines in the product, not six. The stake is the stronger sentence and it must stay true, or it stops being believed.
the age is real11 days comes from the oldest item, recomputed each day.⚠️ It goes up on its own, which is the point: a strip that gets more pointed the longer it is ignored, without ever getting bigger.
the popup is unchangedTapping still grows the box in place — three rows, oldest first, then See all.That part already works. This only changes what the closed box says.
