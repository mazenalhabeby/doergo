15 · Bottom line

Deep paperwork vs a modern platform

PlanD goes very deep into German cleaning paperwork but is limited in flexibility and technology. We are stronger in technology, field operations and working across companies. With payroll, invoicing and languages built, we are a real alternative — even for HBC.
