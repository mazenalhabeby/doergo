④What is on screen now
A BUG, NOT A STYLE
In 11:05 GMT−7 · Out 11:05 GMT−7 · Total —
Three entries on screen have the same in and out time and no total at all — the row simply stops. A member cannot tell whether it counted nothing or whether the app failed to say.
A zero-length entry is real — a mis-tap, a double clock-in. It needs its own words, not a blank.
AND THE REST
✕Three time zones on one screen.BST, GMT−7 and CEST, with no statement of which one is the member's. A timesheet whose times you have to convert is not a timesheet.
✕Six “Done” pills, all identical.White, high-contrast, shaped like buttons, and every one says the same word. Six of the loudest things on the page carrying no information between them.
✕“Sep 25” six times, ungrouped.No day headers and no day totals, so the one question a member has — what did I work that day — needs adding up by hand.
✕Six identical “View activity” links.The row should be the tap target. Six green links down a page is a list of duplicated affordances.
✕A 2m entry looks exactly like a 7h 58m one.Same weight, same size, same colour. The figure is the point of the row and it is the smallest thing in it.
✕“Extra time approvals” sits above Clock In.A leader's queue in the middle of a member's clock screen, above their own primary action.
✕“Clocked Out” as a page heading.It is a state, not a section. And it duplicates what the card above it already says.
✕“Assigned Locations” is a list with no verb.Three places, no distances, nothing tappable that helps. It is reference material parked in the middle of a daily screen.
✕The tab bar covers the last row.Service Center and a “View activity” sit underneath it on two of the three screens.
⑤The rules
THE RULES
one clockThe same card as the home screen. Same shape, same states.shift-clock.md §1 — one shape, every state. Today the home has a designed card and this tab has a heading, a grey dot and a different button. They are the same object.
the month first“My time” answers how much you have worked.So the top figure is the month, with the week under it and the budget as a bar. “You worked 0m today” is the least useful sentence available.
one time zoneEvery time is shown in the member's own zone.The entry's recorded zone is noted on the entry detail, in field, and nowhere else. Nobody should convert GMT−7 to read their own timesheet.
group by dayA day header, a day total, entries beneath.The day total is the answer to the only question the list is asked. It goes in jade, at 13px, right-aligned — the same setting as every other counted figure.
the figure leadsThe duration is the biggest thing in the row.2m and 7h 58m must not look the same. Mono, 14px, right — so the column scans as a column of amounts.
zero is a state“nothing counted”, in warn — never a blank.A mis-tap is real and the record keeps it. Saying so is honest; leaving the total empty makes the app look broken.
flags are quiet⚑ 2.4 km away on the time line, not a banner.It is a note on a record, not an alarm. The full sentence — and who can see it — lives on the entry.
no Done pillA finished entry is the normal case.It needs no badge. The exceptions get the marks; the ordinary rows get the space back.
the row is the tapOne target per entry. No “View activity” link.Everything that was behind that link is on the entry sheet, plus the reason the flag is there.
leaders elsewhereExtra-time approvals moves to ⑤ Needs you.It is a queue somebody else is waiting in, which is exactly what that widget is for. It is not part of clocking in.
