Invoice emails — what they would look like

Two templates in the house style already used by invitations and task alerts, so an invoice email arrives looking like the rest of the product rather than like a different company.

Neither exists yet. “Send” currently writes status = 'SENT' and stops — no mailer, no template, no event anywhere in the invoices module. The client receives nothing, and clientEmail is collected and never used. This page is a proposal, not a screenshot of something running.

The house style, as it exists today

Taken from sendInvitationEmail — 600px, Arial, a blue wordmark over a light card. The two proposals below follow it exactly, so the only new thing is the content.

What building it involves

PieceWhereNote
sendInvoiceEmail() notification-service email.service.ts Eight templates already follow this shape
sendInvoiceReminderEmail() same Takes the days-overdue the list already computes
Emit on DRAFT → SENT auth-service invoice.service.ts Where the status is written today
“Send reminder” action invoices list The obvious next step on an overdue row
A hosted invoice page new public route “View invoice” has to lead somewhere a client can open without an account

The last row is the real decision. A link is simpler than a PDF attachment and lets you see whether the client opened it — but it means a public, unguessable URL per invoice, which is a security surface worth designing rather than adding. A PDF attachment avoids that entirely and is what most clients' accounting departments expect to file.
