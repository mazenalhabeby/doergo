# Privacy Policy — HBCField

**Last Updated:** April 17, 2026

HBC Group ("we", "our", "us") operates the HBCField mobile application and web platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- First and last name
- Organization name
- Role and permissions

### Location Data
- Precise GPS location (only during active work shifts when tracking is enabled)
- Location is used for: task routing, attendance verification (geofencing), live map display for dispatchers

### Work Activity Data
- Task status updates
- Clock in/out times
- Break duration and type
- Service reports (text, photos, signatures)

### Device Information
- Device type and operating system
- Push notification tokens
- App version

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide field service management functionality
- To enable real-time location tracking for dispatchers
- To verify attendance via geofencing
- To send push notifications for task assignments and updates
- To generate service reports and work history
- To improve app performance and fix bugs

## 3. Data Sharing

- We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties
- Your data is shared only within your organization (with admin/dispatcher users)
- We use third-party services:
  - **Expo** (push notifications)
  - **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (push delivery)
  - **Hetzner** (cloud hosting, EU-based)

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- All data is stored on servers located in the **European Union** (Hetzner, Finland/Germany)
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (cost factor 12)
- Authentication tokens are SHA-256 hashed
- Access is restricted by role-based permissions

## 5. Data Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- Location history is retained for 90 days
- Service reports are retained for the organization's record-keeping period
- You can request data deletion at any time

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Object** to processing
- **Withdraw consent** at any time

To exercise these rights, contact: **privacy@hbcfield.com**

## 7. Location Tracking Disclosure

- Location tracking is **only active** when:
  - A technician is clocked in (attendance)
  - A technician is navigating to a task (EN_ROUTE status)
- Location tracking **stops** when the technician clocks out or arrives
- Dispatchers and admins can see technician locations on a live map
- Location data is never shared outside the organization

## 8. Children's Privacy

HBCField is a workplace application and is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes via email or in-app notification.
