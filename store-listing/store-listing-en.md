## Full Description (4000 chars max)
HBCField is a powerful field service management platform that connects your office team with technicians in the field. Dispatch tasks, track locations in real time, manage schedules, and close jobs faster — all from one app.

**For Technicians:**
- View and accept assigned tasks instantly
- Navigate to job sites with one tap
- Submit service reports with photos, signatures, and parts used
- Clock in/out with GPS verification
- Track time off and manage your schedule
- Receive push notifications for new assignments

**For Admins & Dispatchers (Web + Mobile):**
- Create and assign tasks to field technicians
- Track technician locations on a live map
- Monitor task progress in real time
- Manage team members, roles, and permissions
- Review service reports and job history
- Manage schedules, time off, and availability
- Multi-language support (English & German)

**Key Features:**
- Real-time GPS tracking and route visualization
- Push notifications for task updates and status changes
- Service reports with before/after photos and digital signatures
- Attendance tracking with geofence verification
- Break management (lunch, short, custom)
- Invitation-based team onboarding
- Organization settings with badge controls
- Dark mode support
- Offline-ready with automatic sync

**Built for Field Service Industries:**
- HVAC & Refrigeration
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- General Contracting
- Cleaning Services
- Security & Maintenance
- IT Field Services

Download HBCField and streamline your field operations today.
