# Google Play Data Safety Form — Answers

## Overview
- **Does your app collect or share any user data?** Yes
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** Yes
- **Do you provide a way for users to request data deletion?** Yes
- **Data deletion URL/instructions:** Contact support@hbcfield.com or organization admin

---

## Data Types

### 1. Location
- **Approximate location:** Not collected
- **Precise location:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No (only visible within user's organization)
  - **Is it required?** Yes (core functionality: GPS tracking, attendance geofencing)
  - **Purpose:** App functionality
  - **Is it processed ephemerally?** No (stored for route history)

### 2. Personal Info
- **Name:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No
  - **Is it required?** Yes (account registration)
  - **Purpose:** App functionality, account management

- **Email address:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No
  - **Is it required?** Yes (login, notifications)
  - **Purpose:** App functionality, account management

- **Phone number:** Not collected
- **Address:** Not collected

### 3. Photos and Videos
- **Photos:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No (only within organization)
  - **Is it required?** No (optional task attachments)
  - **Purpose:** App functionality (service documentation)

### 4. App Activity
- **App interactions:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No
  - **Is it required?** Yes
  - **Purpose:** App functionality (task status, clock in/out)

### 5. Device or Other IDs
- **Device ID:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No
  - **Is it required?** Yes (push notifications)
  - **Purpose:** App functionality

### 6. Diagnostics
- **Crash logs:** Collected
  - **Is it shared?** No
  - **Purpose:** App stability

---

## NOT Collected
- Financial info (credit cards, bank accounts)
- Health & fitness data
- Messages or emails
- Audio or music
- Calendar events
- Contacts
- Search history
- Browsing history
- SMS/call logs

---

## Data Handling
- **Data encrypted in transit:** Yes (HTTPS/TLS)
- **Data deletion available:** Yes (contact admin or support@hbcfield.com)
- **Committed to Play Families Policy:** Not applicable (not a children's app)
- **Independent security review:** No
